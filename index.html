FAQs

Where can I park?+

Parking will be available in the designated quarry parking area near The Westin Dragonara. Please refer to the Venue page for directions.

Are the ceremony and reception within walking distance?+

Yes. The Sunken Garden and The Reef Club are both within The Westin Dragonara and are only a short walk apart.

When should I arrive?+

If you are attending the ceremony, we kindly ask that you arrive before 18:00. Please note that guests cannot be admitted once the ceremony begins. The reception starts at 19:30.

Can I bring an additional guest?+

To help us honour our arrangements, attendance is limited to the guests named on each invitation. Unfortunately, we are unable to accommodate additional guests or plus-ones who have not been included.

When is the RSVP deadline?+

Kindly reply by 1 May 2027.
